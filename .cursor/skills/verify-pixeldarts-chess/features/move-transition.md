# Move transition

## Sub-features

Piece animation, landed-board hold, and active-player rotation.

## How to get to it (user POV)

Hit a move target.

## Driving it with Dartsnut Agent

Record from the hit through the next board prompt. Confirm that orientation
stays with the shooter during the hold and rotates afterward.

## Gotchas

A final board screenshot cannot prove animation or hold timing.
