# Move targets

## Sub-features

Async analysis, minimum thinking time, unique quality wedges, and three throws.

## How to get to it (user POV)

Complete an opening recap and press A.

## Driving it with Dartsnut Agent

Wait for all targets. Miss twice, then hit a wedge and confirm the selected
move matches its quality.

## Gotchas

Cloud verification uses deterministic ranked moves. It does not install
Stockfish.
