# Opening selection

## Sub-features

Family bands, position bands, legal move history, and recap.

## How to get to it (user POV)

Press A, throw a blue dart at a family, then throw a red dart at a position.

## Driving it with Dartsnut Agent

Use the top band for both choices and confirm the recap returns White to move.

## Gotchas

The fixture book and menu are separate files. A menu id must exist in the book.
